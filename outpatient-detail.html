NHS England's Outpatient Recovery and Transformation Programme is built around three strands: personalised follow-up (e.g. Patient Initiated Follow-Up), referral optimisation (e.g. Specialist Advice & Guidance), and specialty redesign. This page tracks the first two, which have robust national data collections; the third — specialty-level progress — is being sourced directly from Shelford trusts and will be added once available (see note at the foot of the Referral Optimisation section).

Data caveats affecting this page's methodology:
1) Sheffield PIFU (monthly) data stops at August 2025 in the source file — held over with a note throughout, and excluded from the Shelford PIFU monthly trend line to avoid distorting the time series (consistent with how Sheffield is handled on the RTT page). This does not affect the FY2024/25 PIFU utilisation rate below, where a full 12 months of Sheffield data is available.
2) Specialist A&G "Processed" and "Diverted" figures lag behind "Total Requests." England's processed-to-total ratio holds steady at 84–90% every month back to April 2025, then falls sharply to 72.1% in May 2026 — a clear sign May's outcomes are still being reported, not a real change in practice. Total Requests, Pre-referral and Post-referral splits are recorded at request time and show no such drop, so those use May 2026; Processed, Diverted and Diversion Rate use April 2026, the latest month before this lag effect appears.
3) PIFU Utilisation Rate and DNA Rate are FY2024/25 figures from NHS Hospital Episode Statistics (Outpatient, Provider-Level Analysis) — 2025/26 data isn't published until September. These are annual, not monthly, and sit alongside (not blended with) the live monthly PIFU episode counts elsewhere on this page. PIFU Utilisation Rate = FY24/25 PIFU episodes ÷ FY24/25 attended outpatient attendances. DNA Rate = DNA ÷ (Attended + DNA). Three trusts (Birmingham, GSTT, Manchester) appear in the HES file under suffixed codes (RRK-X, RJ1-X, R0A-X respectively) rather than their standard ODS codes — mapped and verified before extraction.
4) England benchmark lines on the trend charts use the national per-provider average scaled to Shelford's trust count (×9 for PIFU ex-Sheffield, ×10 for Specialist A&G), not the raw England total, since Shelford is a small subset of the ~134–140 providers in each file and a direct total-to-total comparison would not be meaningful.

Personalised Follow-Up (PIFU)

Patients initiate their own follow-up appointment when needed, rather than being brought back on a fixed schedule. NHS England's target is for PIFU to reach at least 5% of all outpatient appointments by March 2029.

Sources: PIFU Activity by Month and Provider (latest available month per trust, to May 2026; Sheffield Aug 2025) and Hospital Episode Statistics Outpatient Provider-Level Analysis FY2024/25 (utilisation rate & DNA rate).
